Data Sandbox Guide

Statistics you can grab: a 60-second tour, then everything you need to know.

Narrated tour with guided cursor (0:58). The amber dot follows every click.

01Quick start

  1. Try the demo (no account needed). From the landing page choose Try the demo, or open /?demo=1. Nine flagship modules are unlocked as a guest.
  2. Pick any module card. The portal groups modules by concept. ★ NEXT UP suggests a path; every visible module stays one click away. Deep links work too: ?module=galton-board.
  3. Predict first. Many modules ask what you think will happen — and how confident you are — before they unlock. Answer honestly; the payoff comes next.
  4. Experiment. Drag points, turn dials, stream the balls. When you're done, press "I've experimented, explain what I saw" for feedback keyed to documented misconceptions, plus how your class predicted.
  5. Switch on Mission Mode (amber panel) for graded challenges with live goal tracking — or stay in Sandbox mode forever. Your call, always.

02What makes it different

🎛️ Grab-first simulations

50+ interactive modules from measurement scales to SEM, IRT, and social network analysis. Concepts emerge from manipulation, not lecture.

🌍 Real data with provenance

13 classic open datasets (Galton 1886, Challenger O-rings, Gapminder…), each with a source card: who collected it, why, and how to cite it. Or upload your own CSV.

🔮 Predict–observe–explain

Commit a prediction and a confidence judgment, watch the simulation push back, then get feedback tied to the statistics-education misconception literature.

🤖 Dr. Gem, the Socratic tutor

An AI tutor that reads your live sliders and results — and asks a guiding question before it ever hands you an answer the simulation could show you.

📓 A lab notebook that writes itself

Every prediction, mission, and experiment is assembled into your personal scientific record, with a calibration readout and a printable report.

🧮 An exact statistics engine

Real F, χ², and t distributions, t-based intervals, maximum-likelihood logistic fits — golden-value tested against SciPy. The p-values you see are true.

03The journey

Core curriculum (10 assessments): foundations → visualization → central tendency → normal distribution → hypothesis testing → t-tests → power & effect size → correlation → regression. Advanced track (25 modules): ANOVA to SEM, IRT to topic modeling, each framed by a role you play (program evaluator, test designer, accountable-AI officer…). Research track: a pre-checkpoint, a capstone investigation on data you choose, and a post-checkpoint that measures your growth.

04For learners

Lab Notebook
The book icon (top right) opens your record: predictions with confidence, missions cleared, questions asked, and a Bayesian Knowledge Tracing model — the very one taught in the Knowledge Tracer module — running transparently on your own data to suggest what to study next. Print it as a PDF for your portfolio.

Sounds & motion
Arcade chimes celebrate missions; the speaker icon mutes everything. Reduced-motion settings are respected throughout.

05For instructors

Admin dashboard
Control module visibility and scheduled releases; watch a concept-by-day engagement heatmap and per-module reach in Learning Analytics.

Live Class
Project the anonymous prediction distribution for any gate question while your class commits in real time (5-second refresh) — a ready-made Peer Instruction flow: predict → discuss → re-predict → simulate together.

Checkpoints & consent
The pre/post checkpoints ship with original misconception-keyed items and an explicit research-consent step; swap in a licensed validated instrument (CAOS/BLIS) via data/assessmentItems.ts. Telemetry exports stay deidentified.

06On iPad & tablets (install as an app)

Touch-ready
Every interaction works with your fingers: drag data points, sweep the Z-scanner, tilt the regression line, sort capsules. Draggable charts are scroll-locked under your finger, so the page never scrolls away mid-drag. The layout adapts to portrait and landscape.

Install to Home Screen (PWA)
In Safari: tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen → Add. Data Sandbox opens full-screen as its own app with the bead-curve icon, no browser chrome. On Android/Chrome use Install app from the menu. After the first visit the app shell is cached, so it opens instantly; signing in, class features, and Dr. Gem still need a connection.

Tips
Two-finger scroll works everywhere, including over charts. Sound chimes respect the mute toggle (speaker icon) and your device's silent switch. If a module ever looks stale after an update, pull-to-refresh once.

07Telemetry & privacy

Every interaction is logged for learning-analytics research: clicks, predictions (with misconception tags), mission progress, tutor conversations. Class-level features only ever surface anonymous aggregates. Demo guests are logged as demo-guest. Assessment research use requires explicit opt-in consent, and declining changes nothing about access.
